SOURCES

Poetic Edda and Prose Edda (Thor, Mjölnir, the jötnar, Rán). Grettis saga and Eyrbyggja saga (the draugr). The runes are the Elder Futhark.

HOW IT WAS MADE
ᚠ
ConceptA storm at sea seen from behind Thor, inspired by the Norse sagas: a lone god, a drakkar, and the drowned rising from the waves.
ᚢ
CharactersThor, the draugr and the jötunn were drawn as layered vector illustrations, with soft shading, rim light from Mjölnir and grain texture.
ᚦ
AnimationEvery move is a rigged sprite sheet: idle, three attacks, damage, death, and the draugr walking and burning away under the lightning.
ᚨ
WorldThe sea is built from layered waves with foam, spray and rain, and the boat rocks on the swell as waves break over the deck.
ᚱ
InterfaceScreens are set in Cinzel and Alegreya Sans, with runes in Noto Sans Runic and a cold palette lit by lightning cyan.
